# Bachelor-s-degree-dissertation-Photonic---Simulation-
Topic: Photonic crystal cavity in Terahertz frequency Design

- Designed and performed simulations of one‑dimensional (1D) and two‑dimensional (2D) photonic crystal structures in the terahertz frequency range using Lumerical FDTD software. 
- Optimization of the best structure, including parameters such as the ratio of distances, radius of defects, length and height of the structure, and material selection. 
- Simulation studies. 

Achievements
The final 1D PCC structure revealed a square‑root decay relation between the radius of defects and showed high performance (high Q factor), indicating that the EM wave remains oscillating inside the cavity.

Applications: (6G) Wireless Communications, ultra-low-loss waveguides, optical switches and gas‑particle sensing, etc.
